0.19.0
-------------------------------------------------------------
-Enemy HP Balance Pass: HP adjustments were hand tuned based on beta playtesting data showing most enemy HP was not keeping pace with the player progression curve, especially in HM and SHM. Many enemies only received minor bumps where their TTK was already within a sensible range. Adjustments were also scaled based on whether they're classed as fodder, standard, strong or elite. PHM enemies appearing in HM or later generally got smaller buffs and in some cases may be removed from later spawn pools entirely.

These changes dovetail with a global percent reduction in enemy spawn rates and the spawn cap based on player health thresholds (increased from the public version), though the global rate reductions currently only affect adventure worlds, so the remix world can be tuned separately.

With enemies generally having more HP and the number of enemies encountered decreasing slightly, this should make encounters more impactful rather than the old spawn rates and HP balance which swamped you with tons of enemies that could be burned through rapidly.

These numbers are a first pass. Bumped the beta to 0.19 to reflect the jump in game feel.

0.18.5
-------------------------------------------------------------
-Added Regain system:
Damage taken isn't immediately lost. It displays as a white segment in your health bar, holds for 5 seconds, then drains over 1.5s. Hitting enemies within this window without getting hit again let's you to steal your life back. Get hit before this and your pool is replaced with a new pool. Regain is scaled to take around 4 hits to get your life back, give or take 1-2 based on swing speed. Chip damage from shields and DoT effects reduce your health but do not erase your regain pool. A pool is currently capped at 25% of maximum health, but could be adjusted. Minions and sentries do not offer regain. Whips and all other weapons do.

-Added Experimental Summoner Rework:
Activated via a toggle in the Gameplay config under the Experimental heading. Default is off. Minions and sentries are pulled from their immortal realm and made mortal. They have health and defense, which match your own stats. They block single-hit hostile shots that hit them, but piercing projectiles still pass through. Contact damage (reduced by 80%) and projectiles (reduced by 40%) hurt your minions, but enemies that take contact damage from your minions or sentries deal 40% less damage to you for 5 seconds while ranged hits reduce damage taken by 10%. Hits refresh the timer. Melee/ranged DR does not stack; melee takes priority. This gives ranged and melee minions different tradeoffs, with ranged offering safety at the cost of less upside, while melee fighters have a high risk of dying but offer much greater protection. Using your estus flask heals your minions/sentries, splitting the pool across whichever are hurt. They die in a burst of white spirit dust. Casting costs no mana, keeping the spirit of vanilla 1.4.5's direction, but each minion/sentry has a 90 second cooldown. Resummoning/repositioning is still unrestricted and free, but death will block resurrection till the cooldown has expired. When this happens, the player can summon any other minion/sentry available, as each item has a separate cooldown. This gives the player choices about army composition and replacement, encouraging a greater diversity of minion use. Balance tuning for this experimental mode is all still WIP. Given this mechanic is such a big departure from normal summoner, I'm imagining it might become accessible via an in-game relic stone or something of that kind.
